# Étretat Canvas

The landing piece of the portfolio: a visitor paints Claude Monet's Étretat sunset over Heeyun's own photograph of the same cliff.

## Language

**Photo**:
Heeyun's photograph of the Étretat arch and needle; what the visitor sees before painting.
_Avoid_: Image, picture, base

**Painting**:
Claude Monet's 1883 Étretat sunset; what the Photo becomes where the visitor has brushed.
_Avoid_: Monet layer, artwork, target

**Canvas**:
The surface the visitor paints on, showing the Photo and the Painting in registration at the Painting's own proportions.
_Avoid_: Hero, stage, scene

**Frame**:
The picture frame around the Canvas; the Brush only paints inside it.
_Avoid_: Border, container, card

**Wall**:
The ground the Frame hangs on, which carries the Wall label; it is the site's own dark background, lit around the Frame.
_Avoid_: Background, page, backdrop

**Brush**:
The visitor's pointer while it is over the Canvas.
_Avoid_: Cursor, mouse

**Stroke**:
One mark the Brush leaves on the Canvas; inside it the Painting shows in place of the Photo.
_Avoid_: Trail, reveal, mask

**Registration**:
The alignment of the Photo to the Painting so that the arch and the needle coincide.
_Avoid_: Alignment, overlay, matching

**Painted**:
The state of an area of the Canvas that Strokes have covered; it stays Painted until the visitor starts over.
_Avoid_: Revealed, uncovered

**Wall label**:
The lettering on the Wall beside the Frame that names both works: the Photo and the Painting it becomes.
_Avoid_: Caption, credit, card, plaque

**Reveal**:
Painting the rest of the Canvas for the visitor, when they ask for it.
_Avoid_: Skip, autocomplete, show answer

**Invitation**:
The Strokes the Canvas paints by itself when it opens, Monet's sun and its reflection, to show that the Photo can be painted.
_Avoid_: Hint, intro animation, onboarding

**Still version**:
What a visitor without a hovering pointer, a landscape window or a taste for motion gets in place of painting: the Photo dissolving once into the Painting.
_Avoid_: Fallback, mobile version
